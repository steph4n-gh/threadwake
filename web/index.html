A FIELD GUIDE TO THE LONG WAKE

Repairs are promises.

  1. Find your bearings

    Move with WASD or the arrow keys. Drag to orbit the camera; scroll to zoom.

  2. Approach, then interact

    Move close until an object’s name appears at the bottom of the screen. Press E to work with it.

  3. Restore the first light

    Find A pocket of light and fit it into the Refuge lamp socket. Read the Horn service plate before using the horn.

  4. Shape the circuits

    This browser edition uses authored crew briefings and manual controls. Later, choose labeled calibration spectra and route their sidebands through the chambers. Local AI is in the local edition ↗.

Explore and discover

WASD moves relative to the camera. Drag to orbit; wheel to zoom. Approach a marked object and press E. Read evidence, carry one part, and install it where it belongs. J opens the map; L keeps discovered facts.

Work with the local mind

At a machine, inspect its controls or describe a sequence in your own words. The local model creates a preview you can review before execution. R opens a nearby console or your companion’s radio. Manual controls remain available.

Repair the circuits

Use discovered manual controls, authored signal calibrations and the routing tables. Calibration spectra retain their secondary bands; your routes, allocations and reserve costs determine the outcome. No live model runs in this browser edition.

Make a witnessed agreement

Companions give authored briefings. Discover evidence and inspect the exact agreements shown beside each crew member. The local edition can also match records with a model. Review their costs and later consequences before you countersign.

Carry the consequences

Heat, strain, freight and crew assignments affect later repairs. Essential progress always has an alternative. Signals retain actual local measurements or labeled authored references. Save automatically; falls return you to stable ground.
