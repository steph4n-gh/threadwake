## Audio update qualification

The audio update adds an original 192-second score, procedural world sound and event cues. The source tests use mocked Web Audio nodes to check ownership, exact decoded length validation, codec fallback, deadlines, voice bounds and cleanup; they are not listening tests. All eleven public CPU suites pass.

A desktop Chromium browser exercised gesture unlock, the four volume channels and mute, keyboard access in help, pause/resume, Continue and restart without duplicate music sources, and a real companion cue. Deliberate fixtures separately exercised Ogg failure with actual MP3 decoding and failure of all music assets while movement and interaction remained usable. These fixtures are not full campaign playthroughs.

A browser OfflineAudioContext rendered the actual engine and final encoded assets for a scripted 20-second timeline at game defaults. It included paper, relay, rotor, motor and horn cues, an overlapping cue burst, volume changes, mute, pause and hide/show. The raw float output had no nonfinite or over-range samples; mute/hidden windows reached zero, the voice bound held and disposal cleared nodes. The four files decode to exactly 192 seconds. Original music and revised arrangements went through separate Argon audio critique; model criticism is fallible and does not establish physical-device listening quality.

Two additional 197-second music-only browser offline renders crossed the first loop naturally through Ogg and the deliberate MP3 fallback. Each retained two synchronized sources with stable gains, no seek and no over-range or nonfinite samples. Their exported eight-second joins provide actual post-filter listening material. The MP3 boundary still has a larger local sample step than Ogg; that diagnostic is retained separately from any audible verdict.

Encoded music totals about 12.5 MB including both codec alternatives. Two retained stereo music buffers occupy about 135.5 MB at 44.1 kHz, with additional decoder and graph overhead. This is not a measured peak-RAM cap. Physical headphones/speakers, two uninterrupted audible loops and broad device/browser sound quality remain unqualified. There is no measured quality multiplier.

## Remaining gates

A complete final-build campaign playthrough by an unfamiliar player, a meaningful human hour of play, sustained performance across hardware, physical printing, broad accessibility and full pinned-checkpoint native execution remain open. The opening/manual browser check is a bounded integration test. It does not establish all of those outcomes.

The public GitHub checks and release assets identify the published commit and artifact hashes. Report new results with the exact release/revision, platform, mode and assistance used. Preserve errors, unchanged steering results and yielded attempts as well as successes. Do not turn a file-presence check, authored calibration or archived replay into a claim of fresh inference.
